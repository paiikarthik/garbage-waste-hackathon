How EcoTrack Works

From reporting a trash pile to celebrating a clean neighborhood, EcoTrack makes civic engagement seamless.

1

Snap & Report Waste

Spot illegal garbage on the road? Take a photo. Our AI automatically classifies the waste category (Plastic, E-Waste, Organic) and tags GPS coordinates for municipal follow-up.

stars Earn +10 Impact Points
2

Organize or Join Cleanups

Create community cleanup drives with our AI Event Logistics Optimizer or join existing drives at beaches, markets, and parks with one single click.

Earn Badges & Certificates

Unlock 3D metallic achievement badges, rise to the top of the community leaderboard, and receive verifiable environmental certificates for your contribution.

Platform Capabilities

Built for Impact, Powered by AI

AI Waste Classifier

Pillow-based computer vision extracts color histograms, texture, and brightness distributions to predict waste type with confidence scores.

Interactive OpenStreetMap

Leaflet JS integration visualizes reported garbage hotspots and cleanup drive locations with custom interactive popup markers.

Community wall for sharing zero-waste tips, cleanup updates, likes, comments, and public shareable permalinks for WhatsApp and social media.
